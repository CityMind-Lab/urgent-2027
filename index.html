Call for papers

URGENT brings together researchers in databases, urban computing, and AI to study how agents discover, integrate, query, and analyze urban data, and how data systems can support those workflows.

We welcome research and experience papers, surveys, vision and tutorial papers, benchmarks, tools, and late-breaking results.

Topics of interest

  • Urban data integration

    Schema matching, entity resolution, geospatial grounding, temporal alignment, and multimodal data quality.

  • Database support for urban data agents

    Spatial, temporal, graph, and vector access methods, query optimization across SQL and model calls, streaming, and data freshness.

  • Agentic urban analytics

    Workflows combining retrieval, prediction, causal analysis, simulation, and interactive visual analytics.

  • Trustworthy urban data agents

    Provenance, privacy, access control, uncertainty, execution validation, and human oversight.

  • Benchmarks and tools

    Reusable datasets, geospatial tools, and evaluations of cross-city generalization, spatio-temporal validity, cost, and latency.
